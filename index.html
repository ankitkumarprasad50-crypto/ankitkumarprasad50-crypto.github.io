Ankits Assist

A personal assistant used by one person.

Ankits Assist is a private, self-hosted assistant that helps Ankit Kumar Prasad keep on top of his own email, calendar and work notes. It runs on a server he operates himself and is used by him alone. It is not a product, it is not offered to other users, and there is nothing to sign up for.

What it does

  • Sends him a short morning summary of his calendar and unread mail
  • Sends him an evening summary of what still needs a reply
  • Watches for mail from specific people and tells him when something needs attention
  • Answers questions he asks it directly over a chat app

Which Google data it uses

It reads Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Docs and Google Tasks for the two accounts Ankit has personally connected. It only ever reads and drafts; it does not send email on his behalf.
