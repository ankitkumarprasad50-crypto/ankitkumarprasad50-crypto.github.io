Privacy Policy

Ankits Assist · last updated 12 November 2026

Ankits Assist is a personal assistant operated by Ankit Kumar Prasad for his own use. It has a single user. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and collects no data from anyone other than its operator.

What data it accesses

With the operator's own Google accounts, and only after he grants consent, it accesses:

  • Gmail — reads messages and may create drafts. It does not send, forward or delete mail.
  • Google Calendar — reads events to summarise the day and upcoming meetings.
  • Google Drive and Google Docs — reads and writes documents the operator works with.
  • Google Sheets — reads and writes his own spreadsheets, such as an expenses log.
  • Google Tasks — reads his task list.
  • Email address and basic profile — to identify which account is connected.

How the data is used

Solely to produce summaries, reminders and alerts for the operator, and to answer questions he asks the assistant directly. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, training, or any purpose beyond serving him.

Where the data goes

  • The assistant runs on a private server controlled by the operator. Google credentials are stored encrypted on that server and nowhere else.
  • To produce summaries, the text of relevant messages, calendar entries and documents is sent to third-party AI model providers for processing — currently Ollama Cloud, NVIDIA and Google Gemini. Those providers process the text to generate a response and are subject to their own privacy terms.
  • Summaries are delivered to the operator's own WhatsApp and Telegram accounts.
  • Data is not sold, rented, published, or shared with anyone else.

Retention

Google data is fetched on demand and is not kept in a permanent store. Short-lived logs and summaries live on the operator's server and are deleted as they age out. Revoking the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions immediately stops all further access.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
